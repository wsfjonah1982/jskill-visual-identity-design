# Identity Style Directions

The `styles` menu in `config.json`. Each profile gives a **`style_descriptor`** — the exact phrase
to copy into `brand.json` (every template injects it as `{style_descriptor}`) — plus what that
direction means for the logo, palette, type and imagery. Adapt the descriptor to the
brand; don't use a profile verbatim if the brief pulls elsewhere.

Offer 2–3 directions that fit the brief (with a one-line reason each), not the whole list.

---

## Modern Geometric (default)
- **style_descriptor**: `modern geometric design, clean constructed shapes built from circles and straight lines, balanced proportions, confident flat colour, crisp and contemporary`
- **Logo**: monoline or solid geometric symbol; geometric sans wordmark; consistent stroke.
- **Palette**: one saturated primary + crisp neutrals; one bright accent.
- **Type**: Outfit / Sora + Inter.
- **Imagery**: clean, bright, well-composed; strong shapes and shadows.
- **Fits**: startups, apps, services, modern retail.

## Swiss / International Minimal
- **style_descriptor**: `Swiss international typographic style, strict grid, objective minimal design, bold sans-serif, high contrast, generous white space, no ornament`
- **Logo**: wordmark or simple lettermark in a neo-grotesk; maybe one geometric element.
- **Palette**: black, white + one signal colour (red, blue, yellow).
- **Type**: Inter Tight / Inter.
- **Imagery**: architectural, graphic, cropped tightly; duotone.
- **Fits**: architecture, design studios, cultural institutions, B2B.

## Luxury Editorial
- **style_descriptor**: `luxury editorial aesthetic, refined high-contrast serif lettering, restrained palette, generous negative space, fine detail, quiet confidence, fashion-magazine elegance`
- **Logo**: high-contrast didone or elegant serif wordmark, wide letter spacing, monogram secondary.
- **Palette**: black, ivory, one deep tone (oxblood, forest, navy); metallic as finish only.
- **Type**: Playfair Display / Bodoni Moda + Jost.
- **Imagery**: moody, controlled light, texture, close crops of materials.
- **Fits**: fashion, jewellery, hospitality, premium beauty, real estate.

## Warm Organic / Natural
- **style_descriptor**: `warm organic minimalism, soft natural forms, generous negative space, tactile paper and stone textures, understated premium feel`
- **Logo**: soft-cornered or hand-touched symbol from a natural form; flared or soft serif.
- **Palette**: earthy neutrals + one deep natural colour (sea, forest, clay).
- **Type**: Fraunces + Inter.
- **Imagery**: natural daylight, materials, muted grade.
- **Fits**: wellness, skincare, food, eco, craft.

## Playful & Friendly
- **style_descriptor**: `playful friendly design, rounded chunky shapes, bouncy energetic composition, bright cheerful flat colours, approachable and warm`
- **Logo**: rounded wordmark, maybe a character-like symbol; slight bounce/tilt.
- **Palette**: 2–3 bright colours + a dark ink; high saturation.
- **Type**: Nunito / Baloo 2 + Nunito Sans.
- **Imagery**: bright, people/pets, candid; illustration spot graphics.
- **Fits**: kids, consumer apps, snacks, pets, community.

## Tech / Gradient Futurism
- **style_descriptor**: `futuristic tech aesthetic, sleek precise geometry, luminous gradient accents on deep dark backgrounds, crisp digital clarity`
- **Logo**: abstract or monogram mark; core logo stays **flat** (gradient version as secondary only — I-2).
- **Palette**: deep navy/black + electric blue/violet/cyan; gradients from palette pairs.
- **Type**: Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Sans.
- **Imagery**: abstract light, 3D renders, UI.
- **Fits**: AI, fintech, SaaS, gaming, hardware.

## Heritage / Vintage Craft
- **style_descriptor**: `heritage craft aesthetic, vintage badge and engraving influences, classic serif and script lettering, warm muted inks, letterpress texture, established and trustworthy`
- **Logo**: emblem/badge with est. date, classic serif; simplified secondary mark required.
- **Palette**: deep red/green/navy + cream + muted gold.
- **Type**: Cormorant Garamond / Libre Caslon Display + Source Sans 3.
- **Imagery**: warm film grain, hands at work, workshops.
- **Fits**: breweries, bakeries, barbers, distilleries, family businesses.

## Bold Brutalist
- **style_descriptor**: `bold brutalist graphic design, heavy condensed type, raw grid, stark high contrast, oversized elements, unapologetic and loud`
- **Logo**: heavy condensed wordmark, often cropped/oversized; minimal symbol.
- **Palette**: black, white + one acid colour.
- **Type**: Archivo Black / Anton + Archivo.
- **Imagery**: flash photography, harsh crops, high contrast.
- **Fits**: streetwear, music, nightlife, editorial, activist.

## Japanese Minimal
- **style_descriptor**: `Japanese minimalism, quiet balance, asymmetric composition, ample ma (negative space), natural muted tones, delicate precise linework`
- **Logo**: delicate symbol (enso-like, single stroke), light sans or mincho wordmark.
- **Palette**: off-white, sumi black, one muted natural colour (indigo, moss, persimmon).
- **Type**: Zen Kaku Gothic New / Noto Serif JP + Noto Sans JP.
- **Imagery**: still life, shadows, paper, wood; lots of space.
- **Fits**: tea, ceramics, stationery, hospitality, wellness.

## Retro 70s
- **style_descriptor**: `retro 1970s graphic style, warm sunset colours, groovy soft-serif lettering, rounded stripes and arches, sunny nostalgic optimism`
- **Logo**: chunky soft-serif wordmark, stripes/rainbow arcs, sun motifs.
- **Palette**: mustard, burnt orange, brown, cream, avocado.
- **Type**: Shrikhand / Fraunces Soft + DM Sans.
- **Imagery**: warm film tones, grain, sun flare.
- **Fits**: cafés, surf, records, lifestyle, festivals.

## Hand-drawn Illustrative
- **style_descriptor**: `hand-drawn illustrative style, charming imperfect ink lines, crafted lettering, warm personal character, paper texture, storybook warmth`
- **Logo**: hand-lettered wordmark or drawn symbol — still needs a clean, simple version.
- **Palette**: inky dark + 2–3 soft illustration colours.
- **Type**: hand-lettered logo; body in a friendly sans (Nunito Sans / DM Sans).
- **Imagery**: illustration-led; spot drawings over photos.
- **Fits**: bakeries, bookshops, kids, independent makers.

## Corporate Trust
- **style_descriptor**: `clean corporate design, trustworthy and professional, stable balanced composition, crisp sans-serif, calm blue-based palette, clear and orderly`
- **Logo**: solid symbol (often initial-based) + sturdy sans wordmark.
- **Palette**: navy/blue primary, grey neutrals, one warm accent.
- **Type**: Inter / IBM Plex Sans.
- **Imagery**: real people at work, bright offices, documentary feel.
- **Fits**: finance, insurance, healthcare, consulting, government.

---

## Mixing

Two directions can blend if one leads (`"warm organic minimalism with a Japanese restraint"`).
Never more than two — the descriptor becomes mush, and Seedream averages it into generic.
